## Research Guidelines

1. **Study competitor branding** - How they position and message
2. **Look for gaps** - What competitors don't say/show
3. **Find inspiration** - Great campaigns in adjacent categories
4. **Be distinctive** - Generic positioning = invisible brand
5. **Stay authentic** - Voice must match product reality

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Clear positioning statement
- [ ] At least 3 message pillars with proof points
- [ ] Tone of voice with do/don't examples
- [ ] Visual direction guidance
- [ ] Channel strategy outlined
- [ ] Launch concept presented
- [ ] Competitor messaging analyzed
