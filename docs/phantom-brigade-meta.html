Meta Builds by Mech Role

Community-consensus loadouts distilled from the Steam guides hub, Steam discussion threads, the official Brace Yourself Games wiki, and dev patch notes — every claim below was adversarially fact-checked against its source (18 confirmed, 7 refuted out of 25 verified).

Version note — read first. This is the 1.x-era meta (most guides: Feb–Mar 2023 launch window; Comprehensive FAQ covers v1.2.1; patch 1.3.0 of May 2024 changed weight class, heat, and sniper balance). The game received a 2.0 update on Nov 19, 2025 with a stability rework and "comprehensive weapon balancing" — no quality community guide covering 2.0 meta surfaced yet, so treat exact numbers as directional and mechanics as mostly intact.

Before the builds: the three tradeoffs that define every role

The community meta is less about rigid loadouts and more about how you balance these systems. All three are confirmed against the official wiki and 1.3.0 patch notes. verified 3–0

Weight class = body parts only

Since patch 1.3.0, weight class (stability & collision behavior) is determined only by equipped body parts — not held weapons. A light mech hauling an ultra-heavy weapon no longer gets heavy-armor stability. Pick your chassis for the role, not the gun.

Dash & speed are power vs. mass

Dash distance = thruster power counteracting total mass. Run speed = reactor power counteracting mass. You buy mobility two ways: stronger thruster/reactor, or shedding mass (Light Plate / Hydraulics mods).

Heat: dissipation is king

Heat dissipation sets your sustainable rate of fire; overheat threshold sets how long you can fire before pausing. 1.3.0 added a reactor-dependent heat buffer (heat caps at threshold + buffer) and a Burning status from sustained overheating — reactor choice and heatsink stacking are central to any high-heat build.

Melee / Blade

Knockdown

The community pick is the concussion axe, not the sword. Axes are the only melee weapon with concussion damage — swords cut parts off but don't knock anyone down. Multiple posters converge on this: "sword is very unreliable as it cuts things but doesnt knock down. Axe can knock down enemies"; "concussion axe with concussion mod. 1 tap most mechs." One poster who mains a sword build concedes concussive axes "make things too easy." verified 3–0 · forum consensus

Weapon
Concussion axe + Concussion mod — knocks down / knocks out pilots rather than shaving armor. (Sword alternative if you want style over efficiency: XL Razor.)
Armor
Documented sword-duelist build: full Helge set + Stance shield. Light armor mods in shield & arms, Generator MkIII mods in legs & torso → roughly 14 speed / 34 dash — a fencer that closes fast behind a regenerating barrier. verified 3–0 · single poster
Heavy alternative: Vidar — heaviest set in the game and the best heat dissipation of all heavies (8.8), which is why the community keeps naming it for melee: "Vidar has the best dissipation of all the heavy sets." stat-verified
Heat
A widely-repeated claim that melee attacks generate no thruster heat was refuted 0–3 — don't build around it. Budget dissipation like any other mech.
Caveat
The often-cited endgame axe build (full Vidar, Hotrod reactor, 60–70 dissipation) failed verification 1–2 — treat it as rumor until re-sourced. refuted

Sources: Steam: melee build thread (Mar 2023) · Steam: "Understanding Melee"

Tank / Shield

Protector

Shields are mobile cover, not face-tank tools — and a readied shield raises your effective weight class for collisions, enabling ramming. Three vanilla S-series shields with clean tradeoffs, plus the community-favorite Ajax. verified 2–1

Shields
S-01 Firewall — heaviest, pure Integrity (raw HP wall).
S-02 Stance — trades Integrity for a regenerating Barrier, slightly lighter.
S-03 Tower — lightweight skirmisher shield, least durable; preferred by some for shield-bashing.
Ajax — the "Protector" guide's pick for its low mass: "personally the best of the shields… I wouldn't use it to tank but rather provide cover during maneuvers."
The build
"Protector" mid-game build (ABYSS's Steam guide, updated Mar 2024): AR2 Burst rifle + Ajax shield on full Elbrus body parts. Elbrus chassis is the common element across multiple community shield builds. verified 3–0 · single-author guide
Internals
R6 Reactor (weapon buffs — cuts scatter while moving) · T4 Atlas thruster · 2× Heatsink in the arms to hold dissipation above 40 · Capacitor (heat capacity) + Generator (speed) in the lower body.
Weight
Light Plate + Hydraulics mods to cut mass — this is a shield-bearing flanker, not a damage sponge. High-heat weapons (LMGs) need build adjustments.
Tactics
Dash between cover with the shield raised toward fire; use the raised weight class to shove lighter mechs; apply pressure from the flank while your line holds attention.

Sources: Steam guide: "Protector" Mid-game Build and Flanking (ABYSS) · BYG wiki: Mech Weapons · Steam: "Your Best Builds" thread

Assault / Brawler

Fire on the move

Assault rifles, SMGs, and shotguns (all except the SG1 Auto) have zero scatter penalty while moving — the defining mechanic of the role. They're "invaluable for flankers who can fire without having to stop." verified 3–0

Weapons
Any AR / SMG / shotgun except SG1 Auto (the one exception with a move penalty). The AR2 Burst doubles as the Protector build's primary for the same reason.
Armor
No manufacturer consensus per role — instead the Comprehensive FAQ points at perks: Improved Cooling and Composite are the most generally useful body-part perks. Pick a medium chassis with those and full mod slots (blue quality).
Mobility
This role lives on dash economy: medium weight, strong thruster, enough reactor to keep run speed up. Since weight class is body-parts-only, you can carry a heavy gun without losing your footing.
Tactics
Never stand still — you pay no accuracy tax for moving, the enemy does. Circle to rear/flank arcs and force stationary builds to keep re-facing.

Sources: Steam guide: Comprehensive FAQ & Guide (whythecynic, v1.2.1, upd. Feb 2024) · Steam guide: Weapons, variants and damage types

Sniper / Marksman

Stand still

Patch 1.3.0 made the SR3 Dice the standout rifle — per-hit kinetic damage 120 → 200, range 84–132m → 93–145m, scatter 2–20 → 2–15, concussion 5.1 → 9. Meanwhile the SR1 Midway was nerfed (125 → 110 kinetic). Every number confirmed against official dev patch notes. verified 3–0

Weapon
SR3 Dice — big single hits plus meaningful concussion (pilot pressure). Marksman rifles carry heavy movement-scatter penalties: plant your feet, then fire.
Dissent
The Comprehensive FAQ's author calls sniper rifles "a trap" — ~90m minimum range is restrictive on many maps — and favors the MR2 marksman rifle for the mid-range sweet spot. Worth trying both. attributed opinion
Alternative
From the "Your Best Builds" thread: RA2 Lancer railgun (Supercharge + Liquid Cooling) on a Tsubasa torso with R2 Hotrod reactor (~71 dissipation) — railguns shoot through cover. community post, not verified
Armor
No verified manufacturer consensus for sniper chassis; you're stationary when it matters, so bias toward dissipation perks (Improved Cooling) over mobility.
Tactics
Movement ruins your scatter — position during the enemy's committed actions, then hold still for the shot window. Pair with an assault flanker to punish anyone who dashes at you.

Sources: Official patch 1.3.0 notes · Comprehensive FAQ & Guide · "Your Best Builds" thread

Rocketeer / Missile Artillery

No confirmed meta

Honest result: no missile-build claim survived fact-checking. The widely-repeated "missiles are top-tier because they splash, arc over cover, and track" was refuted 1–2 when verifiers went back to the cited guide. That doesn't mean missiles are bad — it means no quality source actually establishes a missile meta. refuted 1–2

What exists
The weapons guide catalogs ML3 / ML6 / ML10 Hornet launcher variants (arcs, ranges) — good for picking hardware, silent on "best."
Defense
The verified missile knowledge is defensive: counter incoming missiles by breaking line of sight, or dashing just before impact (from the 5-tips video below).
Open question
Best MLS launchers, heat handling, and indirect-fire doctrine remain unanswered by good sources — if you find a solid post-1.3 (or 2.0) missile guide, that's the gap.

Pilot Tactics — any role

5 tips

From the community YouTube guide "How to Win in Phantom Brigade – 5 Top Tips!" — pure tactics, no gear required. verified 3–0 · single source

1 · Analyse
Read enemy actions in detail on the prediction timeline before committing your own.
2 · Missiles
Break line of sight, or dash right before impact.
3 · Focus fire
Concentrate the squad on one target; a dead mech deals no damage.
4 · Angles
Attack from rear and flank arcs.
5 · Stand still
Stationary fire is accurate fire — which is exactly why assault weapons (zero move-scatter) break this rule profitably.

Manufacturer identities — what the stat tables actually support

There is no published community tier list ranking manufacturers per role — a widely-shareable S/A/B/C/F chassis ranking circulating in AI-generated form was checked phrase-by-phrase and matches no Reddit post, Steam guide, video, or wiki. What does exist is real per-manufacturer stat identity, from iceGLider's Body Parts Stats tables, the wikis, and Steam threads. Here is the verified version:

ManufacturerClassVerified identityBest role fit
BeinMedium Highest dissipation of any part listed (9.9 upper); 36% barrier. Slots are defense/mobility — not offensive: "the Tsubasa and Bein are the best mech parts for heat dissipation and heat capacity." Sustained automatic fire — assault / LMG builds that live on cooling
TsubasaLight Highest barrier by far (65%; next best is 50%), lightest mass (21.0), elite dissipation (9.0), very low integrity — "highly mobile glass cannon." Hit-and-run flanker; barrier soaks brief counter-fire between dashes
ElbrusMedium Best medium dissipation after Bein (9.0); the common element across community shield builds. Shield/Protector; all-round medium workhorse
VidarHeavy Heaviest set (31.1 upper), highest health, and best heavy-class dissipation (8.8) — the "runs hot" reputation is backwards. Melee brawler / heavy anchor — mass for collisions, cooling for swing uptime
HelgeHeavy Integrity-leaning heavy (≈4,326 integrity / 14% barrier upper at comparable level); "good balance of Integrity and Barrier." Community-split — one thread literally asks "helge armor just sucks?" Frontline melee (the verified sword-duelist set) / attrition tank
AsgardHeavy Second-heaviest (30.4 upper). Little else verified beyond raw mass. Heavy anchor when Vidar isn't available
ArrowL / M / H Weak cooling (7.0 L / 6.7 M / 4.6 H — the 3H is among the worst listed), but the light line is loved for value: "Arrow Ls are basically budget Tsubasas… Arrow 1L is a favorite chest because of slots and barrier." Budget light barrier builds; avoid for sustained-fire heat
HakobuLight Added in patch 1.2.0 — "specialized light armor preferred by the logistic corps." Torso = defensive, legs = mobility hardpoints. Claims of native range / projectile-speed / armor-pen bonuses are fabricated — no body part in the game grants weapon stats. Defensive/mobility light; role identity still unsettled in the community
Knox · Blackbird— Tabulated in Body Parts Stats but no distinct community-verified identity surfaced. No verified consensus

One structural fact holds everywhere: manufacturers differentiate on dissipation, mass, integrity-vs-barrier ratio, and slot types (offense/defense/mobility per body location) — never on weapon stats like range or penetration. If a tier list assigns a chassis "armor penetration," it's making it up. verified

Sources: Body Parts Stats (iceGLider) · Steam: heat dissipation thread · Fandom: Mech Models · Patch 1.2.0 notes (Hakobu) · stat figures are 2023 "level 18" era — directional post-2.0.

Things you'll read online that didn't survive fact-checking

Each of these appears in guides or forum posts but was refuted when verifiers went back to the cited sources. Don't build around them.

  • 1–2"Missiles are top-tier/overpowered (splash + arc + tracking)" — the cited guide doesn't actually establish this.
  • 0–3"Melee attacks use thrust but generate no thruster heat" — not supported.
  • 1–2"Heavier mechs get bonus melee damage per ton" — the damage-from-mass stat claim didn't hold as described.
  • 1–2Endgame melee = full Vidar + Hotrod reactor + 60–70 dissipation — unverifiable as stated.
  • 0–3"Mass alone determines weight class, speed and dash" — oversimplified; see the primer (power-vs-mass, and body parts set weight class post-1.3).
  • 0–3The PM-01M Cut / PM-02H Line saber descriptions circulating as wiki fact — not on the cited wiki page.
  • 1–2"SR-03 Dice is the concussion sniper / SR-02 Lock is highest damage" (pre-1.3 framing) — superseded and inaccurate as stated.
  • 0–2"Vidar runs hot" — backwards: Vidar has the best dissipation of the heavy sets (8.8), a big part of its melee pedigree.
  • 0–2"Hakobu is the definitive sniper frame with native range/projectile-speed/armor-pen buffs" — fabricated; its hardpoints are defensive (torso) and mobility (legs), and no body part grants weapon stats.
  • 0–2"Bein has offensive module slots" — its slots are defense (arms, top) and mobility (bottom); Bein's real identity is best-in-game cooling.
