Build Overview

The Whirlwind Barbarian is insanely strong and fast. This planner will teach you how to put together all the gear, skills and paragon boards for this iconic skill. This planner will also help you solve all your Fury Management issues, explain aspects that may be a little confusing and cover what weapons to set your skills to. There are several variations in the planner so read through.

The Barbarians this season will all be running some variation of the Melted Heart of Selig + Endurant Faith or Endurant Faith. While this is not a requirement it does make you almost unkillable and the quality of life will go through the roof. Each planner will have variants without it, but this will be your end-game version you're striving for.

    How it Plays

    Whirlwind Rotation

    • Cast Rallying Cry
    • Hold down Whirlwind
    • Use War Cry, Challenging Shout and Call of the Ancients at the same time
    • Cast War Cry and Challenging Shout on cooldown
    • Cast Call of the Ancients every 6 seconds
    • Use Iron Skin as needed for Unstoppable

    Overpower Rotation

    • Cast Whirlwind
    • Cast Rallying Cry
    • Use War Cry and Challenging Shout on packs of monsters or bosses
    • Cast Call of the Ancients once every 6 seconds
    • Use Iron Skin as needed for Unstoppable

    Tower/Push Variant (Resolve)

    • Cast Whirlwind
    • DO NOT use Challenging Shout until you get around a group of monsters. This is needed to build Resolve stacks.
    • Cast Rallying Cry
    • Use War Cry and Challenging Shout on packs of monsters or bosses
    • Cast Call of the Ancients once every 6 seconds
    • Use Iron Skin as needed for Unstoppable

    How it Works

    Build Overview

    This build runs relies on Rallying Cry to give us insane amounts of movement speed and Fury Generation. All the damage we use to increase Whirlwind also increases Tornado Whirlwind. We gain those damage increases from Mighty Roar War Cry, Damage Bonus Challenging Shout and Attack Speed and Damage Bonus Call of the Ancients. All of this combines together for some massive damage!

    Arsenal Selection

    This build will be running on a Dual wield set up. You will need one One-handed Axe and one One-handed Sword. This will be selected for Whirlwind . This will allow you to take advantage of the Two-handed Polearm expertise. We are running Dual-wield for Ambidextrous and a One-handed Axe for Cleaver in the paragon board which is why we run this set up.

    For the Overpower variant, your Arsenal selection will be Two-handed Mace.

    • If you would like to learn how to set your weapon selection watch this video: How to Select Weapons video

    Overpower

    Overpower has changed with the launch of the Lord of Hatred. There is no actual Overpower Damage. We gain extra All Damage by having stacks of Overpower Buff. By default every player can gain 4 stacks. There are ways of increasing that through aspects like Tidal Aspect. It is no longer a multiplier, it acts as a buff to your damage. There are ways of taking that buff and turning it into multipliers with items like Banished Lord's Talisman. The one thing that Overpower does is add a lot of additive damage. This is harder to come by, so it becomes a very valuable resource.

    Depending on how you build your build, you will have several different ways of generating overpower. It will be important to maintain the buff. Your overpower buff will last for 4 seconds and then 1 stack will be removed. If you have something like Blood Boiling Aspect and you pair it with Dominate eventually will have stacks of overpower 100% of the time. This is just one example of how you can gain Overpower stacks.

    If you want to learn the basics about Multipliers watch this video: Additive vs. Multiplicative Damage video.

    How Melted Heart of Selig Overpower Variant Uses Overpower

    This build will give you 4 stacks of Overpower. We are also gaining from Blood Boiling Aspect which is our most important Aspect for this build. Because your stacks fall off one at a time every 4 seconds, every 6 seconds Blood Boiling Aspect adds another rank of Overpower and you will never lose your stacks.

    The Melted Heart of Selig Overpower variant does not use Tidal Aspect because it does not run Banished Lord's Talisman or Banished Lord's Talisman. Tidal Aspect is only worth using if you are running this as a option. This variant does use Overpower for several different buffs. If you would like to use these you can re-arrange the gear to look like the example below. The damage difference is the same if not slightly less but the best part of builds this season is you can play them so many different ways!

      • Tidal Aspect replaces Aspect of Channeling
      • Aspect of Channeling replaces Heavy Hitting Aspect
      • Endurant Faith replaces Gohr's Devastating Grips
      • Banished Lord's Talisman replaces Endurant Faith

    Pit and Tower Resolve Stacking

    This variant gains survivability through the new Resolve stacking mechanic, giving us enough Damage Reduction to drop Endurant Faith or Endurant Faith. To make this work you will need to Temper all Armor and Amulet with Maximum Resolve Stacks. You will need to Masterwork most of these Tempers in order to get enough Damage Reduction. Resolve does not work for all builds or even in all content. For this variant you will rely on Aspect of Dominance to build your Resolve stacks. Challenging Shout is considered Crowd Control when taunting, and will activate Aspect of Dominance to give you stacks of Resolve. You have to pair your Resolve with Aspect of Glynn's Anvil to give you the crazy amount of Damage Reduction from this method.

    Warning

    Resolve cannot be stacked fast enough with this build for it to be used on Bosses. Because they cannot be Crowd Controlled you won't build up enough stacks, which results in having zero Damage Reduction and you will die.

    Vulnerable Damage

    This build uses Ceh to apply Vulnerable (All Variants Except Pit/Tower Push)

    Resolve variant will use Bac + Ohm with Vulnerable War Cry and Exploit. This will give you almost 100% uptime on Vulnerable.

    Ultimate Selection

    We are using Highlander Call of the Ancients because of the significantly reduced cooldown. This allows you to cast Call of the Ancients once every 6 seconds to maintain the attack speed and damage buff. This is still used post patch, even with the bugs to Highlander Call of the Ancients. You still gain the bonus from Attack Speed and Damage Bonus Call of the Ancients by casting Call of the Ancients.

    If you want to learn how to set your defenses up, watch this video: How to avoid getting one-shot video

    Aspect Interaction

    Earthstriker's Aspect give you a damage bonus without swapping weapons. You just need to have the Overpower buff on to activate the second half of the aspect.

    Weapon Master give you a damage bonus without swapping weapons.

    Fury Management

    Affixes

    • Resource Generation will help increase the amount of Fury you gain and pairs really well with Rallying Cry.
    • Fury Regeneration needs to be added onto the Helmet, Chest piece, Pants and Boots.
    • Do not spam Whirlwind if you are not near monsters if you can't maintain Fury.
    • You can also use Tempers that add Lucky hit chance to restore primary resources.
